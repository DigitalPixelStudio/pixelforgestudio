Questions

Still unsure?

How can you deliver in seven days when agencies take six weeks?

Because we removed everything that makes projects slow: no committee, no handoffs between three teams, no waiting on a designer in another timezone. One studio, one design system, one build.

What if I do not like the design?

You see a real homepage before you pay anything. If it is not right, you keep it and we part as friends. On a paid build you get two full rounds of changes at the review stage.

Do I own my website and data?

Your domain, accounts, customer data, hosting, analytics and Google Ads are all in your name. No lock-in, and no fee to keep your own site.

What happens after seven days?

Three months of care is included free: uptime monitoring, backups, security patches and content updates. After month three it continues at ₹2,499 a month, only if you want it.

Do you work with businesses outside Bengaluru?

Yes. Most of our work is remote — calls, screen shares and WhatsApp. We have worked with clients across India.

How do I pay?

Payment is 100% in advance. Work begins once the full amount is received and the package is procured. The mockup stays free, so you see the design before committing.
