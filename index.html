THE 2026 BUILD

How This Site Is Built.

We do not sell what we cannot do. This page is the demonstration — every technique below is in the build you are reading right now.

Rendering

3D Without WebGL

A rotating wireframe globe of 135 nodes and ~200 edges, projected through manual 3D maths on a 2D canvas. No library, no shader compile, no battery drain.

Performance

98+ Lighthouse

Animations restricted to transform and opacity so the GPU composites them. Images in WebP inside fixed aspect boxes so nothing shifts while loading.

Interaction

Cursor Spotlight

Every card carries a glow that follows your pointer, driven by one passive listener writing CSS variables — no per-card handlers, no layout thrash.

Structure

Schema Rich Results

Three structured-data blocks — business, offers and FAQ — so Google can surface your prices and answers directly in search.

Access

Reduced Motion

Every animation respects the visitor's motion preference. Keyboard focus rings on all controls. Alt text on all imagery.

Speed

Under 2 Seconds

Static-first HTML, lazy loading below the fold, font preconnect, and no framework runtime shipped to the browser.

The brief

Fix The Invisible Part.

Red Apple had everything a repair business needs except a way to be found. No website. A Google listing with strong reviews but no link to anywhere. Customers who discovered them by word of mouth and nothing else.

What we built

  • A five-page conversion-focused website — services, pricing, proof, contact — with a WhatsApp button on every page.
  • Google Business Profile setup and optimisation, linked to the new site.
  • Google Ads with full conversion tracking: phone clicks, WhatsApp clicks and form submits each recorded as a separate event.
  • Schema markup and a sitemap, so Google understands what the business does and where.

What changed
