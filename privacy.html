Privacy Policy

How Pixel Forge Studio collects, uses and protects your information.

What we collect

When you contact us we collect your name, business name, phone number and whatever you tell us in your message. When you visit this site we collect anonymous usage data — pages viewed, device type and approximate location — through Google Analytics.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your enquiry and quote for work
  • To deliver a project you have commissioned
  • To send invoices and project updates
  • To improve this website

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data to anyone
  • We do not send marketing messages you did not ask for
  • We do not share client data with other clients

Cookies

This site uses cookies for analytics and to remember your preferences. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

How long we keep it

Enquiry data is kept for 24 months. Project files and invoices are kept for seven years as required by Indian tax law.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email hello@pixelforgestudio.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Third parties

We use Google Analytics, Netlify for hosting and WhatsApp Business for messaging. Each has its own privacy policy. Client project data is stored in accounts registered to the client, not to us.
