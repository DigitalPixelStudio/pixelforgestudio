Why this
studio exists.

Pixel Forge Studio is a Bengaluru studio.

I started this studio because I kept meeting good Bengaluru businesses — a salon in Indiranagar, a clinic in Jayanagar, a repair shop in Indiranagar — who were losing customers simply because they were invisible online. Not because their work was bad. Because nobody had built them anything decent.

Agencies quoted them six weeks and two lakhs. Freelancers disappeared halfway. So we built a studio that ships in seven days, on the same modern stack the big product companies use, and hands over every file, every account, every key.

You own everything. There are no hidden costs. And every site we build includes an AI assistant that answers your customers at 11pm, when you're asleep.

That's the whole promise. Seven days, everything yours.

— Pixel Forge Studio
